## Full description (Play and App Store: 4000)

GdANav is the navigation app for people who drive an electric car. It works for petrol and diesel too.

IT TAKES YOU THERE
Type where you want to go, pick one of the routes and press Start. The voice tells you what to do and when, the next manoeuvre with its exit sign is at the top, and before junctions the right lanes light up in blue. Near the exit the manoeuvre opens into a 3D view of the junction. At the bottom: arrival time, distance left, your speed and the road's limit.

Stops along the way, how to drive (fastest, balanced, economical) and what to avoid: tolls, motorways, ferries. If traffic changes and there's a faster road, GdANav suggests it.

COMMUNITY REPORTS
One tap and you report what you see: traffic, police, accident, hazard, roadworks, road closed, speed camera. Whoever comes after you hears and sees the warning, and can say whether it's still there, so old reports fade away by themselves. Fixed speed cameras in Italy and nearby countries are built in, offline too.

WITH AN ELECTRIC CAR
GdANav works out your consumption leg by leg: speed, climbs and descents, wind, temperature and climate control. It tells you how much battery you arrive with and, if it's not enough, where to stop and how much to charge, with chargers free or busy right now. In "Your car" pick your model from more than 400 electric cars: battery, charging power and plug are already known.

WITH A PETROL OR DIESEL CAR
Pick your fuel and GdANav shows nearby stations with today's prices (Italian Ministry's fuel price observatory): nearest or cheapest, self-service or attended. During the trip too.

AND MORE
• Weather along the trip, at the time you'll be there.
• Android Auto and CarPlay: map, guidance, reports and stops on the car's screen.
• "Hey Siri, take me home with GdANav", "OK Google, navigate to…".
• Offline maps, region by region.
• Home, Work and favourites, imported from your Google Maps saved places too.

HOME ASSISTANT
With the GdANav integration for Home Assistant your car's battery comes from your home, and your home knows about your trip: where you're going, when you arrive, with how much battery. "When I'm about to arrive, open the gate." Data travels encrypted between your phone and your Home Assistant, with no ports to open at home.

FREE AND PREMIUM
GdANav is free to download, with no ads and no account. Routes, voice guidance, lanes, 3D junctions, traffic, speed cameras, reports, weather, fuel stations with prices, offline maps: free. With Premium (€2.99 a month or €29.99 a year, first 14 days free): the battery read from the car (Android Auto, OBD dongle, Home Assistant), routes with charging stops and chargers free or busy, Android Auto and CarPlay. The subscription renews automatically and is cancelled from the store.

YOUR DATA
No account, no ads, no usage statistics. What leaves your phone, and to whom, is in the privacy policy: gdanav.gdahome.org/privacy
